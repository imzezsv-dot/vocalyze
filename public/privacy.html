Privacy policy

What happens to your recording

Written as claims that can be checked, not as reassurance. Each one says what is enforced and where it stops.

The short version

    What this cannot protect against

    Stated because a privacy page that lists only strengths is marketing. The limits are as much a part of the design as the guarantees.

      Consent

      Nothing is processed without the consent box being ticked, and the choice is recorded before the audio is touched. That is a deliberate friction: the people in a recording are not the person uploading it, and a meeting transcript is a record of everyone who spoke.

      Retention

      Everything expires. The window is shown on the result of every run, and erasure is available at any point before it.
