The audit trail

What the system did, and proof it has not been edited

Every action is written to an append-only trail. Each entry carries the SHA-256 of the entry before it, so removing or altering one breaks the chain — and the check below recomputes every hash in front of you rather than reporting a stored verdict.

Actions only. No transcript text is ever written here, which is what makes the trail safe to keep after the transcript itself is gone.
