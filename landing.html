Who said what, and what did you decide?

Two models answer half the question each. Whisper knows what was said and when; pyannote knows who was speaking and when. Vocalyze merges the two clocks into one attributed transcript, then writes a brief in which every point carries the line it came from.

Drop a recording, or choose a file
wav, mp3, m4a, flac, ogg, webm or mp4 · up to 200 MB

Audio is encrypted the moment it lands and deleted once it has been transcribed. The transcript is erased after 24 hours, or now, if you press delete.
